What it does

Favorites, albums, and Shared Albums, full screen, with the house one swipe up.

  • Full-screen photo frame

    Favorites and albums fill the screen, so the photos you love are seen around the home instead of locked away in iCloud.

  • Shared Albums, kept current

    Choose an iCloud Shared Album. When family add a photo, it shows up on the frame on its own.

  • Apple Home, one swipe up

    Swipe up from the frame to control lights, scenes, locks, and thermostats.

  • Nothing leaves your devices

    VidaFrame plays stills from the library on this iPad. Nothing is uploaded.

VidaFrame is free. VidaFrame Plus is an optional upgrade at $0.49 a month. It removes the notice and adds weather.

A frame for the room

Set an iPad where people actually gather. The picture stays full screen. The house stays underneath it, one swipe away, until someone needs a light, a lock, or the thermostat.
