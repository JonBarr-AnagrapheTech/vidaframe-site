Privacy Policy

VidaFrame · Effective October 14, 2026

VidaFrame is an iPad app from Anagraphe Tech LLC. Photos, Apple Home, and location are processed only on the device.

Photos

VidaFrame plays stills from the library iCloud Photos syncs to this iPad, including Favorites, your albums, and iCloud Shared Albums. Photos are processed only on the device. Nothing is uploaded.

Apple Home

Swipe up from the frame to control lights, scenes, locks, and thermostats. Apple Home is processed only on the device. Those controls are not sent to Anagraphe Tech.

Location

Location is processed only on the device.

Weather

Weather requests send an approximate location to Apple WeatherKit, so Apple can return a forecast. That request goes to Apple, not to Anagraphe Tech. Apple describes WeatherKit in Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple. Apple processes payment for VidaFrame Plus. Anagraphe Tech does not receive your payment card.

Accounts, analytics, and tracking

There are no accounts. VidaFrame does not use analytics or tracking. No data is sold.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist on anagraphetech.com, the form sends your email address to Buttondown. We use it only to email you about VidaFrame. You can unsubscribe anytime.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@anagraphetech.com
